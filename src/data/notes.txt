Here is a complete summary of everything discussed for your new sidebar feature:

**Sidebar Placement & Architecture**
You are adding **Kitchen Basics** to the **MENU** section of your sidebar to bridge the gap between finding a recipe and executing it, replacing non-essential pages like About or Contact.

```text
MENU
  ├── Home
  ├── Filter
  └── Kitchen Basics

MY KITCHEN
  ├── Favorites
  └── Own

```

---

**UI/UX Design Concept**

* **Structure:** Clicking "Kitchen Basics" opens a hub organized into **3 primary categories**.
* **Layout:** Inside each category, content is presented as clickable **cards** (featuring a title, brief summary, and icon/preview).
* **Navigation:** Clicking any card opens its full, dedicated page or detailed guide.

---

**Category & Card Content Breakdown**

**1. Essential Calculators & Charts**

* **Measurement & Temperature Converter:** Interactive tool to convert metric/imperial units (grams to cups, °C to °F) and volume to weight.
* **Cooking Reference Cards:** Quick-look lookup tables for safe internal meat temperatures, egg boiling times, and grain-to-water ratios.
* **Recipe Scaler:** Dynamic calculator to scale ingredient quantities up or down based on desired serving size.

**2. Ingredient Swap Guide**

* **Emergency Substitutions:** Quick lookup for common pantry swaps when missing key ingredients (e.g., buttermilk, eggs, heavy cream).
* **Dietary Alternatives:** Easy swaps to make dishes dairy-free, gluten-free, or vegan without compromising texture.
* **Fresh vs. Dried Herbs:** Quick conversion ratios and best practices for substituting dried pantry herbs for fresh ones.

**3. Mastering the Basics**

* **Knife Skills & Prep:** Visual step-by-step guides for basic cuts (dice, julienne, chiffonade) and knife safety.
* **Flavor Balancing:** Core troubleshooting guide on how to fix a dish using salt, acid, fat, and heat.
* **Cookware 101:** Guide on selecting, using, and caring for stainless steel, cast iron, and non-stick pans.
